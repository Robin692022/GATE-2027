Preparation Mindset & Strategy Rules

Rule #1: Quality of Execution

Solving 50 PYQs deeply with full conceptual understanding is 10x better than skimming 200 questions passively. Always maintain a mistake log for every incorrect question.

Rule #2: The Sunday Math Principle

Engineering Mathematics carries 13 marks guaranteed. Reserve every Sunday for Mathematics revision alongside one completed subject. Never skip Math!

Rule #3: Active Recall over Passive Reading

Revising doesn't mean re-reading notes. Close your copy and derive formulas on blank pages or attempt fresh PYQs.

Rule #4: Mock Test Fortitude

Mocks are diagnostic instruments, not ego boosters. Low scores in July/August are blessings if they reveal gaps before February.
