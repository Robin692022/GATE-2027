5. Daily Workflow and Study Modes

5-Step Chapter Workflow

  1. Learn: Watch lectures and make purposeful notes[cite: 3].
  2. Recall: Close notes for 5 minutes and write/explain core ideas & formulas from memory[cite: 3].
  3. Practice: Solve chapter-wise PYQs as soon as chapter is complete[cite: 3].
  4. Diagnose: Log useful errors into the mistake register[cite: 3].
  5. Space: Revisit content within 24 hrs, 7 days, 30 days, and final revision[cite: 3].
Busy College Day (3-4 Hours)
  • Primary Learning (~2 hrs): 1 focused lecture + notes[cite: 3].
  • PYQs (~1 hr): Questions from prior chapter[cite: 3].
  • Recall / Formula (20-30 min): Review 5-10 old formulas & today's content[cite: 3].
Free Day (6-7 Hours)
  • Morning (2.5 hrs): Primary lecture + notes + active recall[cite: 3].
  • Afternoon (2 hrs): PYQs & practice; repair gaps[cite: 3].
  • Evening (1.5-2 hrs): Revision/Formula register + maintenance[cite: 3].

7. Registers System

Formula Register

Limit each chapter to 1-2 pages[cite: 3]. Include core formulas, conditions, unit reminders, and simple diagrams[cite: 3]. Allows refreshing a whole subject in 1 hour[cite: 3].

Mistake Register Categories
  • Concept Error: Misunderstood core idea -> Revisit lecture segment[cite: 3].
  • Formula Error: Wrong relation or unit -> Clarify in formula register[cite: 3].
  • Calculation Error: Arithmetic or sign error -> Apply 30-sec check routine[cite: 3].
  • Careless Reading: Missed word/condition -> Underline conditions during reading[cite: 3].

9. Functional Techniques

Active Recall

Close notes and explain concepts aloud or reconstruct on paper from memory[cite: 3].

Error-Driven Revision

Do not reread an entire chapter for one wrong answer[cite: 3]. Repair the exact failure point[cite: 3].

Assumption-First Thinking

State physical assumptions before selecting equations to prevent formula dumping[cite: 3].

Backlog Buffer

Use Saturday and free-day evenings for spillover[cite: 3]. Do not push the whole plan back[cite: 3].
